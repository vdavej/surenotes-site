Getting Started

From TestFlight to your first setlist in five steps

No account creation, no setup wizard — just install and start bringing in your own charts.

Install TestFlight, then SureNotes

Tap the "Join the Beta" link above on your iPad. It opens Apple's TestFlight app (install it first from the App Store if you don't have it), where you'll accept the invite and install SureNotes.

Create your band

On first launch, SureNotes sets up a default band workspace for you. Everything — songs, setlists, shows — lives inside a band, even if it's just you for now.

Bring in a chart

Import a PDF, a ChordPro (.cho/.pro) file, plain text, or a photo of paper charts (Settings → Bulk Attach, or Scan to PDF from the camera). Don't have any handy? Try pasting in a ChordPro song to see the chord/lyric rendering and transposition in action.

Build a setlist

Add a few songs to a setlist, then attach that setlist to a Show. Attaching freezes a snapshot of the order — the safe, "what we're actually playing tonight" copy that survives later edits to the setlist itself.

Try Performance Mode

Open your Show and tap into Performance Mode — the clean, full-screen view designed to be read from a stand. If you've got a Bluetooth page-turn pedal or an Apple Pencil, this is where they come in.

User Guide

What's actually in the app

A tour of every major area — enough to know what you're looking at, and what's worth putting through its paces.

Library

📚 Library & Import

Your song catalog, organized by band. Import PDF, ChordPro, plain text, and image charts individually or in bulk — SureNotes suggests a matching song by filename so you're not hand-matching dozens of files one at a time.

  • CSV import for bulk song metadata
  • Library Cleanup tool flags likely duplicates and chart-less songs
  • Attach multiple charts and a backing track per song
Reader

📖 Reader

Where you view and mark up a chart outside of a live show — practicing, prepping, or just double-checking a chart before a gig.

  • Freehand ink, shapes, stamps, and text annotations with real Apple Pencil pressure/tilt support
  • Bookmarks and hands-free repeat links (hold your pedal to jump to a chorus, ending, or D.S.)
  • Half-page turning and continuous autoscroll, every chart type
ChordPro

🎸 ChordPro Power Tools

SureNotes parses and renders standard ChordPro natively — chords over lyrics, not just plain text.

  • Non-destructive transposition, capo math, sounding vs. chord-shape key
  • Two-column layout, hide chords/hide lyrics, transposing-instrument support (Bb/Eb/F)
  • Export any chart to a paginated PDF
Shows

🎤 Setlists & Shows

Setlists are your working drafts; Shows are frozen, gig-ready snapshots that stay playable forever, even below your subscription tier.

  • Preflight checklist — catch a missing chart or backing track before you're on stage
  • Quick Song — pull any song into the live running order mid-set without touching the saved setlist
  • Export a show or setlist as PDF, plain text, or CSV
Live

🕹️ Performance Mode

A dedicated, distraction-free view for when you're actually playing — bigger charts, simpler chrome, and every hands-free control this app has.

  • Configurable tap zones for page turns without looking down
  • Bluetooth pedal support: page turns, autoscroll start/stop, custom button assignments
  • Apple Pencil squeeze/double-tap for fast tool switching mid-annotation
Practice

🎯 Tuner & Metronome

Two things every working musician reaches for constantly, built in rather than left to a separate app.

  • Chromatic tuner with cents display, usable from Reader or Performance Mode
  • Metronome with count-in styles matched to real feel (straight, shuffle, 6/8, 12/8) and a "get ready" pre-roll
Data

💾 Backup & Export

SureNotes is local-first — your library is real data on your device, not just a cache of something in the cloud.

  • One-tap full backup to Files, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop
  • Restore replaces your current library from a chosen backup file
  • Song templates and setlist/show exports for sharing outside the app
Testing Program

What we actually need from you

Real usage finds real bugs. The most valuable testing doesn't happen at a desk — it happens at a rehearsal or a gig.

Good testing looks like

  • Bring your own real charts — PDFs, ChordPro files, whatever you actually gig with
  • Build at least one real setlist and attach it to a Show
  • Use Performance Mode somewhere other than your couch — a rehearsal room, a stand, a real pedal if you have one
  • Try the tuner and metronome mid-practice, not just on launch
  • Push on the edges: huge setlists, oddly-formatted ChordPro, low storage, airplane mode
  • Tell us what's confusing, not just what's broken

FAQ

Before you ask

What devices does SureNotes support?

iPad only, running iPadOS 16.6 or later. There's no iPhone or Mac version during the beta.

Is the beta free?

Yes — TestFlight installs are free for the whole beta period, including every Pro feature, so you can put the full app through its paces.

Will my data carry over when the app launches?

Yes. SureNotes is local-first — your library lives on your device — and everything you build during the beta stays in place through updates and into the public launch.

How long does the beta run?

We'll announce the close date on the same channels you found this page through. Testers who stick around for the whole window rank higher for the top 10 rewards.

I don't have a Bluetooth pedal or Apple Pencil — can I still test?

Absolutely. Most of the app doesn't need either. If you do have one, we'd love feedback on that specifically since it's harder for us to test at scale.

What if I find the same bug someone else already reported?

Report it anyway — a second confirmation is genuinely useful. Rewards are based on your own testing depth and report quality, not just being first.

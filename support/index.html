Reporting a problem

These four things let me reproduce almost anything, and including them up front usually saves a round trip:

  • What happened, and what you expected instead
  • Steps to reproduce it — even roughly
  • Your iPad model and iPadOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The SureNotes version (Settings → About, at the bottom of the app)

A screenshot or screen recording helps more than anything else, especially for something visual.

Subscriptions and billing

All purchases go through Apple. I never see your payment details and I can't process refunds — Apple handles both.

What you wantWhere to do it
Cancel a subscriptioniOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions
Request a refundreportaproblem.apple.com
See what you're paying foriOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions
Restore a purchase on a new iPadSureNotes → Settings → Restore Purchases

If a subscription lapses, nothing is deleted. Your library stays exactly as it is. Paid features are restricted until you resubscribe, and the app tells you which ones.

Your charts and data

Everything you put into SureNotes lives on your iPad. There's no account and nothing is uploaded — see the Privacy Policy for detail.

That means backups are worth taking. SureNotes → Settings → Export Backup writes a single file you can keep anywhere: Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a USB drive.

Before a gig that matters, it's worth exporting one.

Moving to a new iPad

Export a backup from the old iPad, then restore it on the new one from SureNotes → Settings. Restoring your purchase is separate — use Restore Purchases, same Apple Account.
